CASE FILE / 001 · PATHVIO

Make the next step
feel possible.

Career techGuided journeysSolo build

Pathvio is an AI career-planning platform for professionals who want to move forward but aren't sure what to do next. The product is designed to turn a broad career goal into a guided sequence of useful actions—not a disconnected collection of tools.

01 / The product problem

Career tools often solve one narrow task at a time. The experience needs to help users select a target role, retain context and understand what action to take after each tool.

02 / Product decisions

  • Use the dashboard as the central decision surface.
  • Make one primary next step prominent.
  • Return tool flows to the dashboard and preserve journey context.
  • Never invent metrics that the backend did not return.

03 / Engineering

React 19, Tailwind and Framer Motion on the frontend; FastAPI and MongoDB on the backend, with Vercel and Render deployment roles documented in the repo. Capacitor wraps the frontend build for mobile.

CASE FILE / 002 · HUSH AI CONCIERGE

Help shoppers choose
with confidence.

Conversational AIDecision UXCommerce

Hush AI Concierge is part of BrandKart, an AI-first footwear demo storefront. It aims to reduce decision paralysis by offering one reasoned top pick, a small number of alternatives and explicit trade-offs grounded in catalog and policy data.

01 / The product problem

A generic chatbot can produce long lists without helping a shopper make a decision. The experience is built around a concise recommendation, transparent reasoning and a direct path back to the product and cart.

02 / Guardrails

  • Ground product and policy claims in known data.
  • Validate model output against catalog truth.
  • Show one top pick and up to two alternatives.
  • Make differences and trade-offs explicit.

03 / Engineering

Next.js and TypeScript storefront; FastAPI and LangGraph conversational pipeline; ChromaDB for product embeddings and policy clauses; Redis-backed session/cache layers documented in the repository.

03 / How I work

Make it useful.
Then make it matter.

Good product work isn't one discipline. It's the decisions between disciplines—and the focus to carry those decisions through to a shipped experience.

I connect user problems, product choices and engineering to create experiences that help people move forward.

My background spans SEO, digital marketing, product growth and hands-on AI product development. That combination shapes how I work: start with intent, understand the journey, find the friction, then build a system that makes the next step clearer.

I'm interested in the space where product strategy meets implementation—where a strong idea has to survive real constraints, where AI needs guardrails, and where a beautiful interface still has to solve an actual problem.

I value clear reasoning, measured claims and useful outcomes over surface-level complexity.
