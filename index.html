02 / How I think

Product is where
everything meets.

Strategy without shipping is a slide. Technology without empathy is a demo. I care about the space between them.

I connect user problems, product decisions and engineering into experiences that create real momentum.

My background spans SEO, digital marketing, product growth and AI-powered product development. That mix shapes how I build: start with intent, understand the journey, find the friction, and design a system that helps people move forward.

I enjoy working across the full arc—from framing the problem and choosing what to build, to collaborating with AI coding tools, validating behavior and improving the experience based on evidence.

I'm especially interested in products where AI can reduce uncertainty, not add another layer of complexity.
